About NJSA

Two decades of practice, built on one motto.

N.J. Suresh and Associates, Chartered Accountants was established in 2003. NJSA's motto is to deliver great results with passion in whatever it does — a highly energetic team that understands your business needs and delivers the right solutions.

NJSA provides a wide range of services that your business needs — from company incorporation and detailed project reports for project financing, to obtaining business licences, managing accounting, payroll, direct and indirect tax compliances, MIS reports, secretarial activities, tax filing, assessments, appeals and quick winding-up.

We also assist with incentive applications under M-SIPS, approvals from the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEF) for import of used electronic equipment, and various DGFT matters — Advance Authorisation, Restricted Licence, Duty Scrips, export incentives, and Import-Export Code.

NJSA has hands-on experience dealing with Income-tax, GST, Service-tax, VAT, Profession Tax, Shops & Establishment, Excise, Entry Tax, Luxury Tax, Customs, DGFT, MoEF, STPI, SEZ, RBI and the Enforcement Directorate.
